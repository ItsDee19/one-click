How to read these results

On “70% win rate” strategies. None of these reaches it, and nothing credible does. These numbers are what each rule actually produced on this universe, not a figure from a trading site. Expectancy is the average profit per unit of risk. Read it alongside win rate and sample size.

The positional rules use years of daily bars; the intraday record is limited to 59 sessions. They cover different horizons and should be read with their own sample windows.
