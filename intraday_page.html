How picks qualify

The engine checks the direction, entry window, observed price, stop, target and remaining reward for each setup. Signal time refers to the bar that formed the pattern; confirmation time is when that completed bar became available.

Strategy evidence is assessed after modeled costs, with separate validation of unseen sessions. Research-only and legacy records remain visible with their limitations. Evidence can support a strategy; it cannot guarantee today's outcome.

Expectancy is the average result per unit of risk. Read it with the cost basis, profit factor, sample window and validation status. Scanned stock counts describe coverage; they are not a count of independent trading days.
